## About

JsonApiToolkit is a lightweight toolkit designed to simplify the implementation of the JSON:API specification in your .NET projects. It streamlines common tasks such as:

- **Mapping** – Automatically converting your entities to JSON:API resource objects.
- **Querying** – Enabling robust filtering, sorting, pagination, and inclusion of related resources.
- **Error Handling** – Standardizing error responses for a consistent client experience.
- **Content Negotiation** – Supporting the `application/vnd.api+json` media type out of the box.

Whether you’re just getting started or are looking for advanced implementation details, this documentation site will help you quickly integrate JsonApiToolkit into your project.
